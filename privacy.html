Iron Front - Privacy Policy

Iron Front collects no personal data.

  • No account or login is needed. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Settings, local high scores and saved replays are stored only on your device (browser local storage). Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes them.
  • Online play is optional and peer-to-peer (WebRTC). Players join with a room code. Game commands go directly between the players' devices. Public signalling relays are used only to connect the players, and no game data is stored on a server.
  • Replays are saved only when you press "Save replay", and they stay on your device unless you choose to share the file.

Third-party code: three.js (MIT licence) and Trystero (MIT licence). All sound is synthesised on the device.

Questions or support: open an issue at github.com/IronForgeCreateGM/iron-front/issues.
