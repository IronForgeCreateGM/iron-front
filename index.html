Game setup
Hold the western plateau against an endless Horde. Waves pour in from the east through four mountain passes — wall them, tower them, survive. Every 10th wave is a boss: clear it fast and clean for a free elite unit. Solo or co-op with up to 4 (friends or ally bots).

How to play
Build Power → Barracks → Ore Refinery → War Factory. The Research Lab unlocks artillery, the Air Command and research tracks (L2 +15%, L3 +30% damage & HP). Harvesters can also be built at the Command Yard. Base wiped out? Any Engineer can found a new Command Center next to itself (same price, the Engineer is used up) — bots do it too. Base wiped out? An Engineer can found a new Command Center (same cost) as long as you have no Command Yard or Center left.
Counters: bullets shred infantry · cannons beat armor · rockets hit armor & air · flak downs aircraft · artillery wrecks bases.
Build Command Centers to expand (bases, production, refineries extend your build radius). Alt-click pings allies. Destroy every enemy base & production structure to win.
