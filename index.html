How to play
Build Power → Barracks → Ore Refinery → War Factory. The Research Lab unlocks artillery, the Air Command and research tracks (L2 +15%, L3 +30% damage & HP). Harvesters can also be built at the Command Yard.
Counters: bullets shred infantry · cannons beat armor · rockets hit armor & air · flak downs aircraft · artillery wrecks bases.
Build Command Centers to expand (bases, production, refineries extend your build radius). Alt-click pings allies. Destroy every enemy base & production structure to win.
