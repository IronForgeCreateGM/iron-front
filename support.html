Iron Front - Support

Iron Front is a real-time strategy game for iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web, published by MBEP Productions LLC.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at github.com/IronForgeCreateGM/iron-front/issues. Please include your device (for example iPhone 15, iPad Air, Mac), the game version (bottom of the title screen) and what happened.

Report a player or a chat message

Online play is only with people in the same private room (you share the room code) or a Game Center Quick Match. The lobby chat is short text only (140 characters), offensive words are masked automatically, and it is off by default in the App Store app.

  • Mute a player right away: tap 🔇 next to their message. Their messages stay hidden on this device (lobby → Unmute all to undo).
  • Turn chat off completely: lobby → 💬 Chat on / 🔕 Chat off.
  • Leave: you can leave a room at any time and host your own private room with only the friends you invite.
  • Report abuse: open an issue at github.com/IronForgeCreateGM/iron-front/issues with the player's name, the room code, the date and time and what was said (do not include your own personal details). MBEP Productions LLC reviews reports and replies there.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Single-player, Endless and Watch modes need no login and work offline. Online play is optional.
  • How do I play online with a friend? Main menu → 🌐 Play Online → Host game, then share the room code. Your friend taps Join with code and enters it (or opens the invite link). Only one of you should host. In the App Store app, Quick Match finds opponents through Game Center and fills empty seats with Hard bots.
  • The game runs slowly on my phone. Turn on Quiet mode (🍃 in the top bar, or M on a keyboard). It caps the frame rate and resolution and saves battery. The huge 8-player maps are the heaviest.
  • No sound on iPhone or iPad? Sound starts after your first tap. Check that the ring/silent switch is off and the volume is up.
  • Restore my Full Unlock purchase (App Store app): Settings → Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. Full Unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • Are my games saved? Settings, high scores and replays are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Controls: on touch screens (landscape recommended) tap the build cards, tap your units to select them and tap where they should go or what they should attack; drag to pan and pinch to zoom. On a Mac or PC: WASD/arrows pan, mouse wheel zooms, drag to select, right-click to move or attack, F attack-move, Ctrl+1-9 control groups.
